### Full description (4000)
Ma Allah is a calm daily companion for the Quran, adhkar and prayer — with no ads, no tracking, and your data kept on your device.

THE QURAN
• The Madani mushaf in the King Fahd Complex script, page by page as printed.
• Recitations by renowned reciters, with each word highlighted as it is recited and pages turning along.
• Verse and passage repeat, and surahs you can download for offline listening.
• Tafsir al-Muyassar and al-Sa‘di built in; Ibn Kathir, al-Tabari, al-Qurtubi and al-Baghawi online; reasons of revelation.
• Search, bookmarks and continue where you left off.

MEMORISE AND COMPLETE
• Memorisation mode: hear a verse with the words lit up, recite it with the text hidden, and review it on a spaced schedule.
• Khatma plans by duration or pages per day, with a daily portion and reminder.
• Group khatma: create one, share the link with family and friends, and everyone takes a juz — no account needed.

PRAYER
• Accurate prayer times for your city with standard calculation methods, adhan and reminders.
• My prayers: log each prayer (in jamaah, on time, late), track prayers to make up and voluntary prayers.
• Qibla direction, Hijri calendar and a Ramadan mode with suhoor and iftar times.

ADHKAR AND DUAS
• Morning, evening, sleep and after-prayer adhkar, one at a time with a counter, virtue and source.
• Hisn al-Muslim, duas from the Quran and Sunnah, the Names of Allah and a tasbih counter.
• Authenticated hadith with grade, source and explanation.

YOUR DAY AT A GLANCE
• A living sky on Home that changes with the time of day, with the sun and moon moving between your prayers.
• My day: one ring that fills with your prayers, Quran and adhkar.
• My harvest: a weekly and monthly card of what you did, to share if you wish.

SOURCES
Quran text from the King Fahd Glorious Quran Printing Complex; recitations and timings from Quran.com; hadith from the Encyclopedia of Translated Prophetic Hadiths (HadeethEnc) and its sources; Hisn al-Muslim by Sa‘id ibn Wahf al-Qahtani. Every hadith is shown with its grade and source.

PRIVACY
No ads, no tracking, no selling of data. Your location is used on your device only, for prayer times and the qibla. Privacy policy: https://ma-allah.vercel.app/privacy
